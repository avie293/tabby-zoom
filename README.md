# Tabby Zoom

This mod adds a simple and smooth zoom to the game.

Tabby Zoom lets you zoom in just like with OptiFine and works straight away without any setup. Zooming in and out is animated instead of jumping, and the camera moves smoothly while you zoom. The mouse gets slower the further you zoom in, so aiming stays easy even at very high zoom levels. This makes Tabby Zoom the perfect companion for building, exploring and scouting from far away.

Hold `C` to zoom in and release it to zoom back out. Scroll the mouse wheel while zooming to zoom further in or out, up to 500×. Hold `F6` for the cinematic zoom, which adds smooth camera movement and black bars at the top and bottom and hides the HUD and your hand, perfect for recordings and screenshots. All settings like zoom level, max zoom, animation speed and bar size can be changed under `Options → TabbyLib → Tabby Zoom`.

**Requires [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) and [TabbyLib](https://avie.cc/wiki/tabbylib/):**

Tabby Zoom is client-side only and works on any server. Report bugs on [GitHub](https://github.com/avie293/avie-issues), the [source code](https://github.com/avie293/tabby-zoom) is on GitHub too.
