# Tabby Zoom

This mod adds a simple and smooth zoom to the game.

Tabby Zoom lets you zoom in just like with OptiFine and works straight away without any setup. It is **client-side only** and **works on any server**. Features include:

- Hold `C` to zoom in and release it to zoom back out, animated instead of jumping.
- Scroll the mouse wheel while zooming to zoom further in or out, up to 500x.
- The mouse gets slower the further you zoom in, so aiming stays easy even at very high zoom levels.
- Hold `F6` for the cinematic zoom, which adds smooth camera movement and black bars and hides the HUD and your hand.

All settings like zoom level, max zoom, animation speed and bar size can be changed under `Options → TabbyLib → Tabby Zoom`.

Pre 2.0.0 versions: [Avies Archive](https://avie.cc/archive/)

Bugs and crashes can be reported in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/avie293/avie-issues/issues) by selecting **Tabby Zoom** as the project.
